Help

Move: Arrow keys / WASD (or on-screen D-pad on touch devices).

Teleporter ■ warps you to its pair. Ice ■ makes you slide. Boost ■ jumps you one extra cell. Slow ■ makes you skip your next move.

Solo editor: click a cell to cycle tiles, right-click (long-press) to erase. Arrow keys pan, C or Rec enter view.

Simulation: 2 to run AIs, SPACE to start, Enter to set time limit.

Spectate: , / . switch who you follow.

Lobby: draw or Generate maze, place your spawn, Ready, then Start (host).

Each button below the map maps to one of the 10 features. Green = free, red = full, blue = your car, gold = pre-booked, purple = EV charging, dark # = locked blocker.

1Book days ahead

Tap "Book Spot", pick a concert and a date, reserve a private spot before you leave home.

2Spot taken? Auto-reroute

If your target spot is occupied, the app finds a new one in under 10 seconds — unless you pre-booked it (then the blocker holds it).

3Heat map

"Heat Map" colours the zones: red = full, yellow = nearly full, green = 10+ spots free.

4Blocker + code

Pre-booked spots raise a heavy physical blocker. It only lowers when you type your unlock code.

5Find your car

"Find My Car" draws a route from your marker back to your exact spot.

624/7 anti-theft

"Simulate Theft" warns if the car moves and you're not with it. It stays silent when your phone (with the app) is inside.

7EV charging spots

"Charging" reveals every charging spot for smart cars when you're running low.

8PRO high alert

"Go PRO" puts the app on very high alert and unlocks extra features.

975% ticket cover

"Ticket Claim" simulates a fine — JustPark covers 75% of it for you.

10GPS pinpoint + guide

Coordinates pinpoint the lot (top-left of the map). A live guide arrow leads you to your spot.

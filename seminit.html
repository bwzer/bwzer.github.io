01The Problem

Malaysia has only ~200 official weather stations. The nearest to Semenyih is at KLIA — 30 km away. A regional forecast cannot tell you when your street is about to flood.

A thunderstorm can flood a taman in 20 minutes

Families get zero warning. Last year Semenyih flooded seven times.

Farmers guess when to harvest

One wrong decision costs RM 500–1,000 in lost crops.

Schools need real wind data

Outdoor sports safety and STEM lessons both need it.

The app says "partly cloudy"

MetMalaysia gives a regional forecast — not your street.

The gap: hyper-local, affordable weather monitoring for Malaysian communities doesn't exist yet.

02The Product

Seminit ("se-minit" = one minute in Malay) is a solar-powered mini weather station that sits on your roof and gives minute-by-minute weather for your exact location.

SensorWhat it measuresWhy it matters
AnemometerWind speed (km/h)Storm warnings, outdoor events, sports safety
Rain gaugeRainfall (mm)Flood alerts, farm irrigation
DHT22Temperature & humidityHeatwave alerts, comfort monitoring
BMP280Atmospheric pressurePredicts rain before it starts — a pressure drop means rain is coming

How it works

  1. The station sits on your roof or garden — the solar panel keeps it running 24/7 with a 3-day battery backup.
  2. It connects over WiFi and sends data to the cloud every minute.
  3. Open the Seminit app on your phone to see live weather for YOUR street.
  4. Get alerts: "Heavy rain expected in 30 minutes" / "Temperature above 35°C — heatwave alert."

10Shark Q&A

What if someone copies you?
Good — that means it works. But I have a head start: 10 schools and 3 taman communities already in conversation, an app built for Malaysian weather, and I'm cheaper — big stations cost RM 10,000+, mine is RM 299.
How accurate is it vs MetMalaysia?
MetMalaysia's sensors cost RM 50,000 each and are built for national forecasting. For what matters to a family — "will it rain on MY street in the next hour?" — Seminit is just as good, with a pressure-drop algorithm that predicts rain at ~85% accuracy.
What if the WiFi goes down?
The station stores 24 hours of data onboard. When WiFi returns, it syncs everything. No data lost.
How do you compete with free weather apps?
Weather apps forecast "Hulu Langat" — 800 square kilometres. Seminit forecasts YOUR taman. The difference between "maybe rain today" and "you have 30 minutes to move your car to higher ground."
Why would farmers pay when weather is free?
Farmers already check the weather — and it's wrong for their specific location. One wrong decision costs hundreds of ringgit. RM 449 + RM 8/month to protect thousands of ringgit in crops is an easy yes.

07Shark Q&A

What happens on cloudy or rainy days?
Panels still generate 25–30% of normal power on cloudy days, and the battery bank stores 2 days of power. On rare 3+ day rain streaks there's a small backup charger. Malaysia gets 5–6 hours of strong sun almost daily.
Why would teens pay RM 9 for nasi lemak when a stall charges RM 2?
They're paying for an Instagram-worthy meal, the feel-good of supporting the environment, and a loyalty program with a free meal every 6th visit. Today's students care about climate.
Can you really make RM 130,000 in Year 1?
85 meals × RM 8.33 × 25 days = RM 17,700/month, minus RM 5,800 costs = ~RM 12,000/month. That's RM 144,000 — I said RM 130,000 to be conservative.
What about food safety and permits?
A food handling certificate (1-day course, RM 100) and a temporary stall license from MPKj. Parents handle cooking and financials; I'm the face of the brand.

Pets escape and get lost

07Shark Q&A

What if the pet chews through the toy?
The electronics are sealed in a separate waterproof hard capsule — the pet can't reach them. If the shell gets chewed, replacement shells cost RM 29 and the tracker moves into a fresh toy in 30 seconds.
How is this different from an AirTag?
AirTags have no temperature sensor, rely on other people's iPhones being nearby (which fails in a taman), and aren't toys — pets won't carry them. Buddy uses real GPS that works anywhere, and pets carry it because it's fun.
What if GPS is weak indoors?
Buddy also has WiFi positioning — near your home WiFi, the app knows the pet is inside. If WiFi drops AND GPS shows movement, that triggers an alert.
Is it safe to chew? What about battery leaks?
The battery is sealed inside the bite-resistant capsule. The shell is BPA-free, food-grade silicone — the same material as baby teething toys.
